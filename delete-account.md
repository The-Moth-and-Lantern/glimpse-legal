# Delete your Glimpse account


Glimpse is made by Moth & Lantern. You can delete your account at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Glimpse and go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Account**, sign in if you need to.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

## Without the app

Email <themothandlanternstudio@gmail.com> from the address you signed in with, with the subject **Delete my account**.
We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted

Your sign-in, email address, player name (`Name#1234`), picture, friends, card collection, all your
decks, the games you recorded, the players you blocked, and any reports about you. Links to your shared
decks stop working.

In games other people recorded, your seat stays as "Deleted player", so their history still adds up.
Reports you sent stay, without your account attached, so they can still be reviewed.

This happens straight away in the app. Copies in the host's routine logs and backups go as those
expire. Your player name can't be recovered, and signing up again gives you a new one.

## What stays

Games, saved players and photos on your phone stay there until you uninstall Glimpse.
