# Glimpse privacy policy


**Effective:** 6 November 2026

Glimpse is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for the Grand Archive trading card game, made by
Moth & Lantern. It is not affiliated with Weebs of the Shore. This page explains what Glimpse
saves about you, where, and how to remove it.

Contact: <themothandlanternstudio@gmail.com>

## The short version

You can search cards, check prices and track games without an account. Glimpse has no ads, no
analytics, and does not sell your data. An account is optional. It holds your email address, your
player name, your decks, your card collection, your friends and the games you save to it, plus
anyone you block and any reports you send.

## If you make an account

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To send your 6-digit sign-in code. There is no password. Other players never see it |
| Your player name and tag (`Name#1234`) and champion picture | To identify you in the app. It's shown on any deck you make public |
| Your decks: names, notes, cards and who can see each one | To keep them in your account, on any phone you sign in on |
| Your games: date, format, who played, who won, final counts, and the deck or champion each player logged | To keep your history and stats on any phone you sign in on |
| Your card collection: which cards you own, how many, your category names, and their finish and condition if you say | To show what it's worth and compare it to your decks, on any phone you sign in on |
| Your friends and friend requests | To let you add each other to games and share decks |
| The players you block | To stop them adding you or seating you in games |
| Reports you send | So the developer can review them (see **Reporting and blocking**) |
| An account ID and your sign-in session | To keep you signed in. The server records the session's IP address and device type while it lasts |

Your decks are private unless you change that. Anyone with the link to a public deck can see it,
along with your player name. Your friends can see friends-only and public decks.

## What other people see

Friends see your player name, tag and picture, never your email. Nobody else sees your collection,
friends included. A game you're in is visible to the
person who recorded it and to everyone else seated in it who has an account. If a friend records a
game with you in it, it only counts for you once you confirm it, and declining removes it for you.

The names in a game are whatever the person recording it entered, so a guest's name you type is
saved with your game.

## Reporting and blocking

You can report a player, or a deck you can see, if it's offensive, harassing, spam or pretending to
be someone. A report saves who sent it, who or what was reported, the reason, any note you add, and a
copy of what was reported as it was then (the player's name and tag, or the deck's name and notes).
Only the developer can read reports. The person you report is never told who sent it.

Reports are kept while they're reviewed, then for up to a year after they're closed, so repeat
problems can be spotted. If you delete your account, reports you sent stay without your account
attached, and reports about you are deleted.

If you block someone, your friendship ends, they can't add you or seat you in games, and games they
recorded that you hadn't confirmed leave your history. They aren't told. You can unblock them in
**Friends** at any time.

## When you search

Card searches are sent to the database to find matches. Glimpse uses what you type to answer that
search and doesn't save it to you or your account.

## Scanning cards

If you use **Scan**, Glimpse asks for your camera. It reads the text on your cards on your phone to work
out which card it is. Pictures from the camera aren't saved, and Glimpse doesn't send them anywhere. You
can refuse, and everything else still works. Google's text reader is part of the app.

## What stays on your phone

The players you save and any photos you add stay on your phone. So do your games if you aren't
signed in, and older games unless you choose to save them to your account. Android may include
these in your own phone backup, apart from photos, depending on your phone's settings. Your sign-in
is stored encrypted and is never backed up.

## Who handles your data

Glimpse doesn't sell your data or share it for advertising. A few services are involved in running
the app:

- **Supabase** hosts the database and sign-in.
- **Google Play services** runs the QR code scanner, if you use it. Glimpse never sees your camera.
- **Google** (Gmail) delivers the sign-in code emails, so it handles your email address and the
  message.
- **Google Play** delivers the app, and Google may give the developer anonymous crash and
  performance figures. Its own privacy policy covers this.
- **Grand Archive's card service** (`api.gatcg.com`) supplies card pictures, so it sees your IP
  address when your phone downloads them, like any website you visit.
- **TCGplayer** opens in your browser if you tap a card's link. Glimpse tells it nothing about you.

These services may keep ordinary server logs, including IP addresses.

All data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept until you delete it. To delete it, open **Settings → Account → Delete my
account**, or follow the steps at [this page](https://the-moth-and-lantern.github.io/glimpse-legal/delete-account). This removes your sign-in, email address,
player name, decks, collection, friends, blocks and the games you recorded from the live database straight away. In
games other people recorded, your seat stays as "Deleted player" so their history still adds up.
Copies in the host's routine logs and backups go as those expire.

Deleting an account doesn't touch what's saved on your phone. Uninstalling Glimpse removes that.

## Children

Glimpse isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you think
a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Your choices

You can see and change your decks in the app at any time, and delete any of them or your whole
account. Your player name can be changed for 24 hours after you choose it. To ask about any other
data we hold, including asking for a copy, email us.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
