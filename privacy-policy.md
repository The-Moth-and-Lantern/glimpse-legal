# Glimpse privacy policy

**Effective:** 5 November 2026

Glimpse is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for the Grand Archive trading card game, made by
Moth & Lantern. It is not affiliated with Weebs of the Shore. This page explains what Glimpse
saves about you, where, and how to remove it.

Contact: <themothandlanternstudio@gmail.com>

## The short version
You can search cards, check prices and track games without an account. Glimpse has no ads, no
analytics, and does not sell your data. An account is optional, and all it holds is your email
address, your player name and your decks.

## If you make an account
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To send your 6-digit sign-in code. There is no password. Other players never see it |
| Your player name and tag (`Name#1234`) and champion picture | To identify you in the app. It's shown on any deck you make public |
| Your decks: names, notes, cards and whether each is private or public | To keep them in your account, on any phone you sign in on |
| An account ID and your sign-in session | To keep you signed in. The server records the session's IP address and device type while it lasts |

Your decks are private unless you make one public. Anyone with the link to a public deck can see
it, along with your player name.

## What stays on your phone
Your game history, stats, the players you save and any photos you add stay on your phone. They
aren't sent to Glimpse. Android may include them in your own phone backup, apart from photos,
depending on your phone's settings. Your sign-in is stored encrypted and is never backed up.

## Who handles your data
Glimpse doesn't sell your data or share it for advertising. A few services are involved in running
the app:
- **Supabase** hosts the database and sign-in.
- **Google** (Gmail) delivers the sign-in code emails, so it handles your email address and the
  message.
- **Google Play** delivers the app, and Google may give the developer anonymous crash and
  performance figures. Its own privacy policy covers this.
- **Grand Archive's card service** (`api.gatcg.com`) supplies card pictures, so it sees your IP
  address when your phone downloads them, like any website you visit.
- **TCGplayer** opens in your browser if you tap a card's link. Glimpse tells it nothing about you.

These services may keep ordinary server logs, including IP addresses.

All data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Keeping and deleting your data
Your account data is kept until you delete it. To delete it, open **Settings → Account → Delete my
account**, or follow the steps at [this page](https://the-moth-and-lantern.github.io/glimpse-legal/delete-account). This removes your sign-in, email address,
player name and decks from the live database straight away. Copies in the host's routine logs and
backups go as those expire.

Deleting an account doesn't touch what's saved on your phone. Uninstalling Glimpse removes that.

## Children
Glimpse isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you think
a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Your choices
You can see and change your decks in the app at any time, and delete any of them or your whole
account. Your player name can be changed for 24 hours after you choose it. To ask about any other
data we hold, including asking for a copy, email us.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Upcoming versions of
Glimpse add synced games and friends, and this page will be updated before they arrive.
