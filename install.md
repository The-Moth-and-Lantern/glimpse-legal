# Install and update Glimpse during testing

Glimpse is in a private test on Google Play, so it won't show up if you search for it. You'll need
an Android phone running Android 8.0 or later, and an invite.

## Install
1. **Ask to join.** Send your Google Play email address to the Moth & Lantern developer by direct
   message on Discord. Please don't post it in a channel.
2. **Open the test link.** Once you've been added, you'll get a link or you can find one posted in Discord #Welcome. Open it on your phone, signed
   in to Google with that same email address, and accept the invite.
3. **Install.** Follow the link to Glimpse's Play page and tap **Install**.

If the link says the app isn't available, your phone is probably using a different Google account.
Switch accounts in the Play Store, using your profile picture, and open the link again.

## Update
New test versions come through Play like any other app.

- **Automatic:** if your phone updates apps by itself, there's nothing to do.
- **By hand:** in the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then **Manage apps & device**, then
  **Updates available**. Or open Glimpse's Play page and tap **Update**.

A new version can take a few hours to reach you. Release notes are posted on Discord.

## Don't uninstall to fix an update
Your games, stats and saved players are only on your phone, so uninstalling can erase them. Your
account and decks are safe, because they're saved online.

Only install Glimpse from Play. A copy from anywhere else won't update from Play, and you'd have to
uninstall it first.
